# iPAS study site visual build brief

> This brief governs the authenticated study experience across home, topic practice, timed exams, results, and the wrongbook. Product truth remains in `PRODUCT.md` and `.scratch/ipas-ai-prep-site/spec.md`; the approved visual authority is `.impeccable/mocks/ipas-topic-catalog.png`, with `.impeccable/mocks/ipas-study-home.png` and `.impeccable/mocks/ipas-timed-exam.png` as companion screens. Updated 2026-09-29.

> Acceptance target: desktop browser. Mobile compatibility is outside the user's current requirement.

THESIS: A numbered study catalog turns every syllabus section into a direct route to questions, with source and topic always traceable. Use the user's approved shared visual system: warm newsprint, near-black ink, precise editorial rules, and restrained vermilion return marks. No generic AI imagery, invented score claims, or decorative dashboard metrics.

STORY: The learner opens the home page to find weak topics and start targeted practice. In practice, a compact course index leads to one selected topic, its explanation, and related questions. During timed exams, the question and choices dominate; timer and question index remain findable, while source metadata stays collapsed. Results explain answers and offer an explicit choice to save a missed question or concept.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On desktop topic practice, a compact horizontal header spans the page. A vertical rule separates a narrow L21/L23 syllabus index on the left from the selected topic on the right. The selected topic title and plain guide link sit above its explanation; related questions and one vermilion practice action follow. Preserve the geometry and wording in the approved comp for the hero phase.

FORM: Numbered question catalog, previously selected by the user; direction seed `9901b926`. Companion home and timed-exam compositions carry the same palette, typography, rules, and numbered navigation while showing the user-prioritized weak-topic and exam-reading hierarchies.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
